# About Staxless

## Who We Are

**Staxless** is a SaaS boilerplate designed to accelerate your journey from idea to launch. Created by [Zachary Benson](https://twitter.com/zacharybenson), we’re passionate about empowering developers, startups, and entrepreneurs to build scalable, subscription-based applications without reinventing the wheel.

## Our Mission

We believe that creating a SaaS product should be fast, fun, and accessible. Staxless combines cutting-edge technologies—Next.js, Node.js, Stripe, and more—into a ready-to-use framework that saves you time and lets you focus on what matters: your unique idea.

## What We Offer

- **Speed**: Go from zero to a fully functional SaaS in hours, not weeks.
- **Scalability**: Built with microservices and Docker for seamless growth.
- **Flexibility**: Customize everything—frontend, backend, and integrations—to fit your vision.

## Why Staxless?

The name "Staxless" reflects our goal: to strip away the complexity ("stacks" of setup) and deliver a lean, powerful starting point. Whether you’re building a side project or a full-fledged business, we’ve got your back with:
- Secure authentication via Lucia and OAuth.
- Stripe-powered payments for subscriptions or one-time sales.
- A beautiful, marketing-ready frontend with blogs and docs.

## Meet the Creator
