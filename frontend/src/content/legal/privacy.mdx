# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: [Insert Date]

At **Staxless**, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use our SaaS platform and services ("Services"). By using Staxless, you agree to the terms outlined here.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

### Personal Information
- **Account Data**: When you register, we collect your name, email address, and any additional details you provide (e.g., during OAuth login with providers like Google or GitHub).
- **Payment Data**: For subscription payments, we collect billing information via Stripe, though we do not store your full payment details ourselves.
- **Usage Data**: We may collect metadata about how you interact with our Services, such as IP addresses, browser type, and session timestamps.

### Non-Personal Information
- **Analytics**: Aggregated data about usage patterns to improve our Services (e.g., page views, feature usage).
- **Cookies**: We use cookies to manage sessions and enhance your experience (e.g., remembering your login state).

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:
- Provide and maintain the Services (e.g., user authentication, subscription management).
- Process payments and fulfill orders through Stripe.
- Communicate with you about updates, support, or promotional offers (you can opt out anytime).
- Analyze usage to improve functionality and user experience.
- Ensure the security of our platform (e.g., detecting fraudulent activity).

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:
- **Service Providers**: Third parties like Stripe (for payments) and MongoDB (for data storage) under strict confidentiality agreements.
- **OAuth Providers**: When you log in via Google or GitHub, we share minimal data to authenticate you.
- **Legal Obligations**: If required by law, we may disclose data to comply with regulations or protect our rights.

## 4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard measures to protect your data, including:
- Encryption of sensitive information in transit and at rest.
- Secure session management via Lucia and MongoDB.
- Regular security audits of our infrastructure.

However, no system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection against unauthorized access.

## 5. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data.
- **Update**: Modify your account information via the dashboard.
- **Delete**: Request deletion of your account (subject to legal retention requirements).
- **Opt-Out**: Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact us at [support@staxless.com](mailto:support@staxless.com).

## 6. Third-Party Services

Our Services integrate with third parties (e.g., Stripe, Google). Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, which we encourage you to review:
- [Stripe Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our Services evolve. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of Staxless after changes implies acceptance of the new terms.

## 8. Contact Us

Questions or concerns? Reach out to us:
- **Email**: [support@staxless.com](mailto:support@staxless.com)
- **Website**: [staxless.com](https://staxless.com)
